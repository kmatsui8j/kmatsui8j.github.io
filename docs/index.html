Researcher · Doctoral student

Kyoko Matsui (松井 京子)

I study procurement systems and contractual governance for large, technically complex public projects.

My research uses game theory, contract theory, and comparative institutional analysis to examine investment in specifications, vendor lock-in, contract changes, and dispute resolution. I am a doctoral student in the Department of Urban Management, Graduate School of Engineering, Kyoto University, and a JSPS Research Fellow DC2.

Topics and current work

Research

  • Public procurement and contract design
  • Early Contractor Involvement (ECI)
  • Incomplete contracts and investment incentives
  • Contract changes and dispute resolution
  • Defence and public construction procurement, international industrial cooperation
  • Contract theory, risk management, and civil engineering planning

Research in progress

Contractual Governance of Public Works: Evidence Production and Monitoring in the UK and Japan

With Masamitsu Onishi. Manuscript in preparation; first and corresponding author. Compares incentives for evidence production and monitoring in UK and Japanese approaches to contract changes and dispute resolution. Intended journal: Group Decision and Negotiation.

Incentives for Joint Design Investment in Early Contractor Involvement

First and corresponding author. Theoretical analysis of buyer and contractor investment in specifications and the role of remuneration. Conference paper submitted to the 44th JSCE Construction Management Symposium. Presentation is not yet confirmed.
